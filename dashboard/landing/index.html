Firefighters can't see what they're breathing.

Toxic VOCs build up in rooms long before smoke looks dangerous, and exposure adds up across a career.

02Every breath mapped.Each gas reading is stamped on the map where it was taken.

03Exposure alerts before you'd notice.Banner outside, ring on the map, the moment the air turns.

04Predicts gas in rooms nobody's entered.Striped means predicted. The ring is the likely source.

05Replay the whole walk.Every position and reading, scrubbable, from the time-series store.
